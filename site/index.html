Get started

Up and running in a few minutes

You'll need your Canvas web address (the one you sign in at, like canvas.youruni.edu) and a minute in Canvas's settings.

Download and open it

Pick your computer below and open the file you download.

A warning on first launch is expected. Tarpaulin isn't signed with an Apple or Microsoft certificate yet. On Windows choose More info → Run anyway. On a Mac, right-click the app and choose Open.
Connect Canvas
  1. In Canvas, open Account → Settings.
  2. Under Approved Integrations, choose + New Access Token and create it.
  3. Paste it into Tarpaulin with your Canvas address, and choose Test & connect.
Stuck? The sign-in screen has the same steps under How do I get a token?
Make it yours

Pick a look, start from a ready-made layout, and add, move or resize widgets until it fits how you work. Tarpaulin keeps itself up to date from then on.

Private by design

Your account stays yours

Read-only

Tarpaulin can only look at your Canvas account. It can't submit, post, edit or mark anything.

Your login stays locked away

Your Canvas token lives in your computer's own password store, never in Tarpaulin's files.

Nothing leaves your computer

Your notes, checklists and layouts stay with you. Tarpaulin only talks to your own Canvas, and to GitHub to check for updates.

Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. Tarpaulin is free and open source software, with no ads, accounts or trials.

Will it work with my school's Canvas?

Yes, if you can create an access token in your Canvas settings (most schools allow it). If yours doesn't, your Canvas admin can set up the "Sign in with Canvas" option instead.

Is it made by Canvas?

No. Tarpaulin is an independent project, not made by or affiliated with Instructure or Canvas LMS. It uses Canvas's normal, read-only access for students.

Does it work on a phone or tablet?

Not yet. Tarpaulin is a desktop app for Windows, Mac and Linux computers.

What happens when I'm offline?

It shows what it last loaded, for up to two weeks, with a note saying when it was updated, and catches up when you're back online.

How do updates work?

Tarpaulin checks for a new version once a day. When there is one, a banner appears; choose Update now and it reopens just where you were.

Can I move my layout to another computer?

Yes: Settings → Export on one computer and Import on the other.
